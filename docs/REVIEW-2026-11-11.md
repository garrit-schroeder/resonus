## B1 — `downloadGroup` guard is a TOCTOU: the group key is not claimed until 18 lines later  `⚠ BEHAVIOR`

`src/store/downloads.ts:998` checks `if (get().active[groupKey]) return; // already in progress`,

## B2 — Podcast feed body read is outside the deadline — a stalled publisher hangs the screen forever  `⚠`

`src/api/podcasts.ts:100` builds an `AbortController`, `:101` arms the 20 s timer (`TIMEOUT_MS` at `:67`), `:104` passes `signal` to `fetch` —
but reads the body at **`:123` `body: await res.text(),`** with no race against `untilAborted(ctl.signal)`.
`src/api/deadline.ts:11` documents exactly why that is not enough: "`text()` and `json()` are read natively, and that read does not hear the signal".

(and the `finally { clearTimeout(timer) }` at `:132-134` never runs, so the handle stays armed). `subsonic.ts:604`/`:605` and `jellyfin.ts:234`/`:288` DO race this; podcasts does not.

`Verify:` `grep -n 'const ctl = new AbortController();\|body: await res.text(),' src/api/podcasts.ts` → 100, 123; `grep -c untilAborted src/api/podcasts.ts` → 0.
Certainty: **certain**. Fix: `await withDeadline(TIMEOUT_MS, …)` (deadline.ts already exists), or race `res.text()` against `untilAborted`.
`⚠` turns an infinite hang (dead screen, no error, offline detection never hears it) into a `FeedError('http')` after 20 s.
